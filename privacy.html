Effective October 7, 2026

Privacy policy

This policy covers Hermes Backup, a personal, non-commercial rclone Google Drive backup app operated by its owner, rrbbbq, and this informational website.

Data accessed and purpose

Hermes Backup uses the Google Drive API with only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. In this rclone setup, access is limited to files and folders created by the app. It handles encrypted Hermes backup archives, checksum files, and their associated Drive metadata, such as file names, IDs, sizes, and modification times.

This data is used only to create, verify, maintain, and restore the owner's backups. The app does not request full-Drive access, Gmail, contacts, or other Google data scopes.

Storage and credentials

Backup archives are encrypted before upload and stored in the owner's Google Drive. Checksum files and Drive metadata may be stored separately from the encrypted archives. OAuth access and refresh tokens are kept in rclone configuration on the owner's authorized computers or VPS and used only for this Drive connection. Encryption keys remain under the owner's control.

This website and its public GitHub repository contain no backup data, OAuth tokens, client secrets, or encryption keys.

Sharing and limited use

Hermes Backup does not sell Google user data or disclose it to unrelated parties. Data is transferred to Google Drive as the storage provider and to the owner's devices for backup and recovery. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models.

Hermes Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion, and revoking access

Backups remain until the owner deletes them or removes them under their chosen backup retention settings. The owner can delete the app's files from Google Drive and remove local backup copies and rclone credentials from their devices.

The owner can revoke the app's access in Google Account third-party connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete files already stored in Google Drive or on the owner's devices.

This website

This is a static GitHub Pages website. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking scripts, loads no external assets, and offers no forms or sign-in. GitHub, as the hosting provider, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Website visits do not grant access to Google Drive.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the repository's issue tracker. Do not post credentials, backup contents, or other private information in public issues. Policy changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
